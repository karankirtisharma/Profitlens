# Synthetic showcase — May–November 2026

Every customer, amount and contract here is fictional and labelled for demonstration.

The hosted **ProfitLens Studio — Synthetic demo** is preloaded. To demonstrate a new import, select that workspace and upload `new-import-invoices.csv` in Imports → Invoices → Preview → Confirm. It adds one November invoice for S01, INR 17,700 including INR 2,700 tax. Repeating confirmation/import must not duplicate it.

`customers.csv`, `invoices.csv`, `expenses.csv` and the four PDF contracts are the seed inputs. On a new empty synthetic workspace, import customers first, then invoices and expenses; upload each PDF to its matching S01–S04 customer and review terms. Do not mix these with a real-data workspace.

The separate real-data demonstration files are in `data/private/dwp-2026`, not this folder. See `docs/PLANNING_AND_SHOWCASE_2026-11-03.md` for provenance, limits, matching and test evidence.
